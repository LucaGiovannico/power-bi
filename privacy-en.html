Privacy Policy

Information on the processing of personal data

This page explains which personal data is collected through the website, for which purposes it is used, and which rights you can exercise.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. Data Controller

The data controller for the personal data collected through this website is:

Dina Fiorentino

Email: Dina Fiorentino Consulting

Website: https://www.dinafiorentino.com

For any request regarding the processing of personal data, you can contact me at the email address indicated above.

2. Personal data collected

The following personal data may be collected through the contact form on the website:

  • first and last name;
  • email address;
  • phone number or WhatsApp contact, if provided;
  • company name, if provided;
  • main goal selected in the form;
  • content of the message sent;
  • any additional information voluntarily entered by the user.

The website may also process technical browsing data, such as IP address, browser data, device information, and technical information necessary for the proper functioning of the website.

Providing the information marked as required in the form is necessary to submit and manage the request. If this information is not provided, the request cannot be submitted or answered.

Optional information, such as a phone number or company name, may be omitted without preventing submission of the request.

3. Purposes of processing

Personal data submitted through the contact form is used exclusively to:

  • respond to the request sent by the user;
  • evaluate a possible professional collaboration;
  • manage any subsequent communications related to the request;
  • comply with any legal obligations.

The data is not used for newsletters, profiling, personalized advertising, or unsolicited promotional communications.

4. Legal basis for processing

The processing of personal data is based on:

  • pre-contractual steps taken at the user's request, when data is used to respond to enquiries, quotation requests, or potential professional collaborations;
  • compliance with legal obligations, where processing is necessary to comply with applicable laws or requests from competent authorities;
  • the controller's legitimate interests, limited to website security, abuse prevention, technical management of systems and protection of the controller's rights, with due regard for the rights and freedoms of individuals.

5. Processing methods

Personal data is processed using IT and telematic tools, adopting appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure.

6. Recipients and third-party services

The website uses Formspree to manage the contact form. When a user submits the form, the information entered is transmitted to Formspree to receive and manage the request.

Formspree may process such information on behalf of the controller, according to its contractual terms, security measures and privacy notices.

Data may also be processed by hosting providers, email service providers and technical service providers strictly necessary for the functioning of the website and the management of communications.

Depending on the processing involved, these parties may act as processors or independent controllers for their respective activities.

7. Transfers of data outside the European Economic Area

Some technical service providers used by the website may process personal data in countries outside the European Economic Area, including the United States.

Where applicable, these transfers are made on the basis of adequacy decisions, Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, or other safeguards provided by applicable law.

Individuals may request further information about the safeguards applied by contacting the controller at the address indicated in this Privacy Policy.

8. Data retention

Data submitted through the contact form is retained for as long as necessary to respond to the request and manage any subsequent professional relationship.

If no collaboration or further need arises, requests and related communications are retained for a maximum of 12 months, unless legal obligations, disputes or the need to protect the controller's rights require otherwise.

To the extent under the controller's control, this retention period also applies to copies held in email systems and platforms used to receive requests.

9. Data disclosure

Personal data will not be sold, transferred, or disclosed to third parties for commercial purposes.

It may be disclosed only to parties that support the controller in the technical management of the website, the contact form, hosting, email, or other services strictly necessary to manage the requests received.

10. Data subject rights

Where provided for by applicable law, individuals may request:

  • access to their personal data;
  • rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • erasure of their data;
  • restriction of processing;
  • objection to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, where applicable;
  • information on safeguards relating to international transfers.

To exercise these rights, please write to: Dina Fiorentino Consulting.

The controller will respond within the time limits established by applicable law. Individuals also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.

11. Automated decision-making

Personal data collected through the website is not used for automated decision-making, profiling, or decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects on individuals.

12. Cookies and similar technologies

For information on the use of cookies and similar technologies, please see the Cookie Policy.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated over time. Any changes will be published on this page with an indication of the last update date.
