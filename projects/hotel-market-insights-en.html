Context

Understanding a market in constant evolution

Hospitality is shaped by seasonality, dynamic pricing and increasingly fragmented booking channels. To properly evaluate hotel performance, looking at revenue alone is not enough: occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, lead time and channel distribution must be connected into a single analytical view.

Business Challenge

The critical issues to solve

The 2024 analysis highlights three critical areas: declining revenue, dependency on intermediated channels and pressure on profitability.

Revenue Decline

Year-over-year revenue decline required a clear understanding of the root causes behind the contraction.

OTA Dependency

The strong weight of intermediated channels may affect margins and reduce commercial control.

Margin Reduction

RevPAR decline required integrated monitoring of ADR, occupancy and profitability dynamics.
