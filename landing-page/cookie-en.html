2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on a user’s device during browsing. They can be used to make the website work properly, remember certain preferences, collect aggregated statistics, or measure visits.

Technologies similar to cookies may include pixels, tags, scripts, local storage, or other tools that make it possible to store or read information on the user’s device.

3. Types of cookies

  • Technical or necessary cookies: they are required for the proper functioning of the website and do not require user consent.
  • Functional cookies: they allow the website to remember user preferences, such as language or browsing settings.
  • Analytics cookies: they help understand how users interact with the website, for example which pages are visited and from which devices.
  • Profiling or marketing cookies: they are used for advertising, remarketing, or tracking user behavior across multiple websites.
